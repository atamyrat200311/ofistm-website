01Our story

We started with reports and ERP integrations. We still start every project with how the business runs.

ofis.tm began in 2017 in a small office in Ashgabat. Our first projects were reports and integrations for companies running Logo ERP: getting the numbers out of the system and in front of the people who make decisions.

Clients kept asking for more. A website connected to their stock. An app for sales reps. A tool for export paperwork. We grew into a full software team, and kept the habit of understanding the process before writing the code.

Today we're designers, engineers and ERP consultants working with manufacturers, distributors and retailers in Turkmenistan and abroad.

02Where we are

Based in Ashgabat. Working worldwide.

Local clients get a team they can meet in person. International clients get a partner that knows the regional market, its regulations and its ERP systems.
